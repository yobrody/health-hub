# Health Hub — Brand & Audience Foundation (v1)

_Date: 2026-08-20. Status: foundation locked; two items parked (final name, icon)._

> This is the brand spine for **Release 1** — a lean, luxury, mainstream consumer health app (home + gym + nutrition). The avatar/shop/social/battles world is a **separate, later app** (see the master plan). Everything below is the "why/feel"; the master plan is the "what/when".

## The Apple principle (the through-line)

We do not target a demographic. We make something so tasteful and so good that **everyone wants it**; the targeting is invisible. Demographics are *design inputs*, never the message. The brand speaks a universal aspiration — beauty, calm, honesty, "it just works."

## Audience

- **Quiet center of gravity — "the elevated adult" (persona B).** Upper-middle/upper, lives the Erewhon / lululemon / Oura lifestyle. Health = status + longevity + self-care. Values trust, calm, quality; pays for premium that feels considered; time-poor, wants clarity at a glance. She anchors our taste, quality bar, and willingness-to-pay — but we **never name or pander to her**.
- **Cultural carrier — "the wellness-native" (Gen Z).** Aesthetic-first, wellness-as-identity, allergic to fakeness and to guilt-y cluttered trackers. Gives us culture and virality. Rides along; never pandered to.
- **The bridge:** they're the same psychographic at two life stages — aspirational, aesthetic-driven, wellness-native, and **hungry for honesty** in a category full of fake numbers and AI slop. The unifying want: *wellness as a calm, beautiful, honest daily ritual.*
- **Cultural moment:** the premiumization of wellness (Oura/Whoop/Alo), the soft-wellness aesthetic, and a real backlash against gamified guilt + fake data → an opening for **honest, beautiful** health.

## Positioning

- **Category:** not "a calorie counter" or "a gym log" — **the honest, beautiful home for your body**. A premium personal-health companion.
- **The enemy:** cluttered, shaming, **fake-number** trackers (spreadsheets that guilt you) and clinical/bro fitness apps.
- **The defensible difference:** **honesty** (never a fabricated number — the engineering principle *is* the brand) × **beauty/calm** × **effortless**. In a category of guesses and guilt, we tell the truth, gorgeously.
- **Promise / soul-line — PARKED.** Working candidates: _"Your body, beautifully understood."_ · _"Honest, beautiful health."_ · _"Health that tells the truth."_ Do not finalize yet.

## Brand personality & voice

- **Personality:** calm, warm, confident, honest, understated. A trusted friend with impeccable taste who never lectures — Apple's restraint + a human pulse.
- **Voice rules:** clear over clever · warm not cutesy · honest even when it's "we don't know yet" · encouraging, never shaming · short confident sentences · quietly premium.
- **Voice in the wild:**
  - ❌ "You blew your goal! 😱" → ✅ "300 over today. Tomorrow's a clean slate."
  - ❌ fake precision → ✅ "Not enough to say yet — log a couple more days."
  - ❌ confetti spam → ✅ (silence, then) "That's a PR." only when it's real.

## Visual identity (direction — a dedicated design pass will detail it)

- **Light mode — "Creamsicle":** ambient hero = a **soft creamsicle orange**, warm and gelato-soft, *never bright or loud*, settling into a **soft warm white** secondary canvas. Warm charcoal text; restrained accents.
- **Dark mode — "Obsidian":** deep, **matte, layered near-blacks** with a faint warm undertone. Depth via **tonal elevation + soft shadow, never a glow**. Orange appears **desaturated/burnt and matte**, used sparingly. Sleek, heavy, luxurious — obsidian, not OLED-black-with-highlighter.
- **Light & dark only** (no themes); both must "scream luxury" through warmth + restraint.
- **Icon — PARKED.** Direction: a **single piece of produce, abstracted and refined** (confident, minimal, almost logo-like — not photoreal, not cute), telegraphing natural/honest/nourishing. Final form deferred.
- **Type:** warm **serif** for hero numbers/headers (editorial luxury) + clean **geometric sans** for UI; big confident numbers, generous whitespace (Apple-Fitness scale).
- **Materials & motion:** soft depth, buttery 120fps springs, tactile haptics. Luxury is *felt* — smoothness + restraint, not decoration.

## Experience principles (used · feels · plays)

1. **Glanceable calm** — the home answers "how am I / what's next" in one look; depth one tap away.
2. **Effortless capture** — a meal/set/weigh-in is ~2 taps; the app does the math.
3. **Honest by default** — "—" over a guess; disclosed estimates (~); trust *is* the product.
4. **Delight, earned & rare** — a ring fill, an honest PR moment; restraint makes them mean something.
5. **Quiet intelligence** — one gentle, useful nudge, never a stream of alerts.
6. **The hidden door** — the future game world sits behind a subtle rift on the home screen: invisible to the mainstream who want calm, a secret for those who want play. It never compromises the luxury base.

_Performance as luxury: instant, smooth, offline-resilient — "it just works."_

## Name brief (criteria — we choose later)

The winning name must: evoke the **warm, cream-orange, natural/nourishing** world · read **premium & universal** (Apple-desirable), not niche/cute · be **ownable** (trademark-clear in Health & Fitness, clean App Store name, `.com`-or-close domain) · be **short, global, easy to say/spell** · **extend to the sequel game app** · sit in **no other brand's shadow**.

### Ruled out (availability/trademark)
- **Clementine** — prominent Apple-featured wellbeing app + likely trademark in wellness. (User's favorite feel; captured via the creamsicle palette instead.)
- **Apricot** — existing food/ingredient scanner app (nutrition-adjacent).
- **Marigold** — multiple health apps ("Marigold Health", "Marigold: Self-Confidence").
- **Satsuma / Marmalade** — clear, but user rejected the feel.
