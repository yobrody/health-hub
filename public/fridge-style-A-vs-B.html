Fridge style: A vs B

Pick the visual direction. Both will use Noto Color food icons inside (these mockups use simple shapes as placeholders).

Option A

Front cutaway

Classic children's-book fridge. Front view, door appears "see-through" so you read items as if looking through the door panel.

  • Easy to read at a glance — symmetric, familiar
  • Items in clear shelf rows, no perspective math
  • Magnets, brand badge, dial all visible up front
  • Less "3D" — no door swung open
  • Can't show door bins (egg holder, etc) — they'd be hidden
Option B

Door swung open

3D-feeling perspective. The fridge body is straight; the door is drawn opened to the left, showing the inside of the door with bins.

  • Reads as a real fridge with the door OPEN
  • Door bins (egg holder, condiments, bottles) actually visible on the door
  • 3D depth via the side panel + parallax door
  • Wider — takes more horizontal page space
  • More elements to maintain across edge cases
Reply with A or B in chat once you've decided. Both will get the full Noto cartoon treatment for items + bigger size + auto-grow shelves with item count.
